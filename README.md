## World Time Zone Meeting Planner

Add up to 8 cities from a dropdown of 40 major cities worldwide to see their current local times with live clock updates. A 24-hour timeline highlights business hours (09:00-18:00) for each city in a distinct color, with a green overlap row showing when all cities are simultaneously in business hours. Click any time slot to see all local times at that UTC hour.

**Tech:** Vanilla JS, Tailwind CSS CDN, Intl API for timezone-accurate time display.

**How to use:** Select cities from the dropdown and click "Add City". View live clocks on city cards, then scan the timeline for green overlap slots. Click any column to inspect all local times for that UTC hour.
